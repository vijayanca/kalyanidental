Treatments

Every treatment starts with an examination and a clear explanation of the options, the number of visits and the cost.

Extractions and disimpaction

Removal of badly damaged or decayed teeth, and surgical removal of impacted teeth such as wisdom teeth, under local anaesthesia.

Restorations

Tooth-coloured fillings to repair cavities and chipped or worn teeth, restoring their shape and function.

Missing tooth replacement

Dental implants and fixed bridges to replace one or more missing teeth, so you can chew and smile comfortably again.

Root canal treatment and crowns

Treatment of an infected or painful tooth to save it instead of removing it, completed with a crown chosen from different crown types.

Smile designing

Planned improvements to the shape, shade and alignment of your front teeth, worked out together with you before treatment begins.

Orthodontic treatment

Braces and clear aligners to straighten crowded or spaced teeth and correct the bite, for children and adults.

Clinic and equipment

The clinic is equipped for digital diagnosis and comfortable treatment.

  • Two electric dental chairs for comfortable, adjustable positioning
  • Portable and wall-mounted X-ray units for X-rays at the chair
  • RVG digital X-ray sensor for instant images on screen
  • 3D intraoral scanner for digital impressions without impression material
  • Ultrasonic scaler for cleaning and removing tartar
  • LED light-cure unit for setting tooth-coloured fillings
  • Autoclave sterilisation of instruments, with distilled water
